## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? Describe the problem, the
constraints (technical, organizational, time), and the forces in tension.
State facts and the assumptions you are making. Avoid arguing for a solution
here — just frame the problem so a reader who arrives in a year understands
*why a decision was even needed*.

## Decision

The change we are making, stated in the active voice: "We will …".
Be specific and concrete. If you considered alternatives, name them and say
in one line why each was rejected — the rejected options are often the most
valuable part of an ADR.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include both the good and the bad:
new constraints introduced, follow-up work created, things that are now
forbidden or now possible, and how this decision will be revisited or
falsified. Note any migration/rollout steps if the decision changes runtime
behavior.
